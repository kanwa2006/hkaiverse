## Reconciliation With Course Map

All codes, titles, and credit values visible in the screenshots match `COURSE_MAP.csv` and `CURRICULUM_AUDIT.md`.
Specific notes:

- **HS101** — shows 0 credits in the portal (Semester I). This matches the dataset.
- **HS301** — shows **0 credits** in the Semester V portal screenshot. This confirms the student-supplied instruction to record HS301 as 0 credits. No discrepancy exists with the screenshot.
- **IP201** — labelled "ST-II / Internship-I" on the portal. The student confirms this is IP-1 (the mandatory two-month internship after Semester IV, worth 3 credits).
- **PH101** — shows **5 credits** in the Semester II screenshot. The proposal reduces this to 4 credits; the screenshot confirms the baseline value.
- **Semester VIII (IP-2)** — not shown in any screenshot. IP-2 (Mandatory Internship-II, 15 credits) is not part of the regular portal view; it is the six-month final internship. No course code is specified in the supplied materials.

No semester VII or VIII screenshot showing additional courses beyond CS415, DS306, DS311, DS401, and MG401 was supplied.

## What These Screenshots Do and Do Not Establish

**What they establish:**
- The course codes, titles, and credit units visible in the portal for Semesters I–VII and IP-1.
- That HS101 and HS301 are shown as 0 credits in the portal.
- That PH101 carries 5 credits in the baseline.
- The academic year assignment for each semester (2023–2024, 2024–2025, 2025–2026, 2027).

**What they do not establish:**
- The official IFHE batch-specific curriculum regulation or syllabus document.
- Officially mandated prerequisites.
- Assessment weightings or formal course regulations.
- Details of courses not visible in the portal (IP-2 internship, elective tracks, if any).
